## Tips for Best Results:

1. **Use Gemini Image Generation** or **Google ImageFX**
2. **Aspect Ratio:** 
   - Hero: 16:9 (landscape)
   - Services: 3:2 (landscape)
3. **Style:** Choose "Digital Art" or "Photorealistic" based on preference
4. **Colors:** Emphasize blue (#2563eb) and white
5. **Quality:** Select highest quality/resolution option
6. **Download:** Save as JPG format
7. **Resize:** Use online tools if needed to match exact dimensions

---

## Quick Generation Steps:

1. Go to: https://gemini.google.com or https://aitestkitchen.withgoogle.com/tools/image-fx
2. Copy one prompt at a time
3. Paste and generate
4. Download the best result
5. Rename to exact filename (hero-bg.jpg, service-1.jpg, etc.)
6. Place in: `public/images/home/` folder
7. Refresh website to see images
