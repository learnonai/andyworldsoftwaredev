## QUICK GENERATION GUIDE

1. **Go to:** https://gemini.google.com or https://imageai.google.com
2. **Copy prompt** for each image
3. **Generate** and download
4. **Rename** to exact filename
5. **Place** in correct folder:
   - Portfolio → `public/images/projects/`
   - Blog → `public/images/blog/`
   - Logo → `public/images/logo/`

---

## TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

- **Style:** Choose "Digital Art" or "Photorealistic" 
- **Quality:** Select highest resolution
- **Colors:** Emphasize blue (#2563eb) and white
- **Format:** Save as JPG (photos) or PNG (logos)
- **Resize:** Use online tools if needed
